#### RLF - Redundant Liquidity Feature


#### Description


The contract implements an auto-generated liquidity pool mechanism. The fees that are


going to the liquidity pool are always zero. As a result, the entire liquidity pool mechanism is


redundant.


#### Recommendation


The team is advices to either remove the liquidity poll feature or add some fees to the


`liquidityFeeonBuy/liquidityFeeonSell` variables.
