####  High Severity Issues

###### 1. Burn issue

**Issue:**

###### • With each transfer to a burning wallet, its balance increases, but the

total supply decreases. After a certain number of such transactions,
the sum of the balances will not be equal to the total supply.

**Recommendation:**

Revise burn logic of the contract and keep only one way of burning –
decreasing total supply, or sending tokens to zero address.
