#### MT - Mints Tokens


#### Description


The contract operator has the authority to mint tokens. The owner may take advantage of it


by calling the `mint` function. As a result, the contract tokens will be highly inflated.


#### Recommendation


The team should carefully manage the private keys of the owner’s account. In addition, the


owner must be mindful and verify that the reward calculation mechanism is functioning


properly and not generating an excessive amount of tokens.
