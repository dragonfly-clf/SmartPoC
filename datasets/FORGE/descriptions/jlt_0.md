#### ST - Stops Transactions

**Criticality** Critical

**Location** JLT.sol

**Status** Unresolved

#### Description

The contract owner has the authority to stop the sales for all users excluding the owner, as

described in detail in sections `PTRP` and `PVC. As a result, the contract might operate as`

a honeypot.

#### Recommendation

The team is strongly encouraged to adhere to the recommendations outlined in the

respective sections. By doing so, the contract can eliminate any potential of operating as a

honeypot.
